# CubeSat 4-Channel Distributed MPPT & Electrical Power System (EPS)

A flight-ready 4-channel distributed Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT) battery charging and power distribution board designed in KiCad for CubeSat nanosatellite missions. Engineered for the Queen's Space Engineering Team (QSET) satellite branch.

---

## Key Features

* **4x Distributed Synchronous MPPT Buck Chargers (LTC4162)**:
  * 4 independent MPPT tracking channels (1 per CubeSat body face), eliminating partial shading and thermal mismatch losses across orbit.
  * Autonomous Constant-Current / Constant-Voltage (CC/CV) power-path management charging a 2S Li-ion battery bus (~8.4V nominal).
  * Phase-synchronized 1.5 MHz switching frequency via MCU clock distribution buffer to prevent harmonic beat frequencies and reduce EMI.
* **Microcontroller & Rad-Tolerant Storage Core**:
  * **STM32L496 ARM Cortex-M4**: Ultra-low-power MCU handling housekeeping telemetry, fault recovery, and bus communications.
  * **4 MB Radiation-Hardened MRAM**: Immune to Single-Event Upsets (SEU) and Total Ionizing Dose (TID), preserving black-box flight telemetry across power resets.
  * **4-Pin High-Vibration Crystal Oscillator**: Superior mechanical stability against rocket launch vibrational loads compared to traditional 2-pin crystals.
  * **Deterministic Hardware Watchdog (TPS3823A)**: Hardware supervisor with active-low reset and Schottky diode isolation protecting against SEU lockups in LEO.
* **Satellite Bus Communications & Auxiliary Power Path**:
  * **Dual Redundant CAN Bus Transceivers**: Conforms to European CubeSat standard bus communication architecture.
  * **I2C Bus Multiplexer (TI TCA9548A)**: 8-channel I2C switch addressing 4 identical LTC4162 chargers at fixed I2C addresses.
  * **Dual Synchronous Buck Converter (LTC3633)**: Monolithic dual synchronous buck generating regulated +3.3V and +5.0V system rails (<15 mV ripple).
  * **Autonomous Power-Path & Cold-Start (LTC4412)**: Ideal diode controller providing seamless fallback between battery and direct solar harvest for dead-battery bootstrapping.
* **Power Telemetry & Comprehensive eFuse Protection**:
  * **INA3221 Triple-Channel Current/Voltage Monitor**: Digital I2C telemetry reporting bus voltages, currents, and power consumption.
  * **TPS25947 Precision eFuses**: Dedicated protection on output rails with programmable current limits (1.33 A clamp on +3.3V), slew rate inrush control (5 V/ms), transient blanking (0.44 ms), hardware UVLO/OVLO windowing (3.02 V – 3.63 V), and remote MCU/OBC shutdown.
* **Form Factor & Mechanical Integrity**:
  * Standard PC/104 CubeSat stackable form factor.
  * Dedicated battery interface with 4-wire Kelvin sensing and NTC thermistor inputs.
  * 6-Layer stackup with solid ground reference planes, top-side RF/switching hot loops, and vibration-resistant soft-termination MLCC capacitors.

## Technical Specifications

| Parameter | Specification / Value | Engineering Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Solar Input Channels** | 4 independent MPPT channels | 1 channel per satellite face (up to ~18V $V_{\text{oc}}$ per string) |
| **Battery Bus Configuration** | 2S Li-ion (6.0V – 8.4V) | Direct autonomous CC/CV charging |
| **MPPT Switching Frequency** | 1.5 MHz (Synchronized) | Phase-locked via MCU PWM buffer to eliminate beat frequencies |

| **Bus Interfaces** | Dual CAN Bus + I2C | Redundant CubeSat standard bus communication |
| **PCB Stackup** | 6-Layer FR4 (1.6mm) | Top: Switching, L2: GND, L3: Mix, L4: Mix, L5: GND, L6: Digital |
| **Mechanical Standard** | PC/104 CubeSat Standard | Custom battery interface with Kelvin sense + NTC |

## Power Telemetry & eFuse Configuration

Each regulated rail is protected by a TI TPS25947 eFuse and monitored by a TI INA3221 current/voltage telemetry IC:

* **Current Limit Threshold ($I_{\text{LIM}}$)**:
  $$I_{\text{LIM}} = \frac{13.33}{R_{\text{ILIM}}\ [\text{k}\Omega]} = \frac{13.33}{10\text{ k}\Omega} = 1.33\text{ A}$$
* **Output Slew Rate & Inrush Control ($\frac{dV}{dt}$)**:
  $$\frac{dV}{dt} = \frac{500}{C_{dV/dt}\ [\text{pF}]} = \frac{500}{100\text{ pF}} = 5\text{ V/ms}$$
* **Overcurrent Fault Blanking Timer ($t_{\text{ITIMER}}$)**:
  $$t_{\text{ITIMER}} = C_{\text{ITIMER}}\ [\text{nF}] \times 0.2 = 2.2\text{ nF} \times 0.2 = 0.44\text{ ms}$$
* **Voltage Window (UVLO / OVLO)**:
  $$\text{Operational Window: } 3.02\text{ V} \le V_{\text{IN}} \le 3.63\text{ V}$$

## Hierarchical Schematic Structure

The hardware schematic is split into modular hierarchical sheets:

| Schematic Sheet | Description | Primary Components |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `EPS_mppt_board.kicad_sch` | Root top-level hierarchical sheet | Interconnects and top-level bus routing |
| `MPPT_IC.kicad_sch` | Quad MPPT top-level sheet | 4x instances of `MPPT_indv.kicad_sch` |
| `MPPT_indv.kicad_sch` | Single-channel MPPT buck charger | LTC4162, power inductors, input/output caps |
| `3.3V.kicad_sch` | Dual synchronous buck converter | LTC3633 (generates +3.3V and +5.0V rails) |
| `MPPT_3.3V_logic.kicad_sch` | Power-path and bootstrapping | LTC4412 Ideal Diode Controller |
| `Sensing.kicad_sch` | Power telemetry and fault protection | INA3221 monitor, TPS25947 eFuses |
| `STM32L4.kicad_sch` | Microcontroller, MRAM, and clocking | STM32L496, 4MB MRAM, 4-pin OSC, 1:6 PWM buffer |
| `I2C.kicad_sch` | I2C bus multiplexer | TCA9548A 8-channel I2C switch |
| `External Watchdog IC.kicad_sch` | Deterministic hardware supervisor | TPS3823A watchdog IC + Schottky pull-up |
| `PC104.kicad_sch` | Satellite bus interface & connectors | PC/104 bus header, battery Kelvin sense, solar inputs |
